Put the cooked ModActor files in this folder:

    ModActor.uasset
    ModActor.uexp

and any other cooked assets the mod uses that live in the same Content\Mods\{{PakName}} folder of your editor project
(widgets, textures, other Blueprints), keeping their sub-folders.

They come from your editor project, after cooking:

    <EditorProject>\Saved\Cooked\WindowsNoEditor\<EditorProject>\Content\Mods\{{PakName}}\      (UE4)
    <EditorProject>\Saved\Cooked\Windows\<EditorProject>\Content\Mods\{{PakName}}\              (UE5)

pack.bat leaves this text file out of the pak.
