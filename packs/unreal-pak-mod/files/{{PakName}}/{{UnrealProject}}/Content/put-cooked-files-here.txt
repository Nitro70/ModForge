Put the mod's files in this folder, at the same path they have in the game.

This folder stands for the game's {{UnrealProject}}\Content folder, which Unreal calls /Game/.
A game asset /Game/Characters/Hero/T_Hero_D is the files

    Characters\Hero\T_Hero_D.uasset
    Characters\Hero\T_Hero_D.uexp       (and .ubulk when the asset has one)

and its replacement goes here as

    {{PakName}}\{{UnrealProject}}\Content\Characters\Hero\T_Hero_D.uasset
    {{PakName}}\{{UnrealProject}}\Content\Characters\Hero\T_Hero_D.uexp

The files must be cooked for the game's engine version ({{EngineVersion}}): raw .png or .fbx files do nothing.
pack.bat leaves this text file out of the pak.
